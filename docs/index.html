two binaries, one repo

They import each other. Split them and both break.

Left pane is the agent. Right pane is the door: Headroom, LiteLLM, spend, CLI versions. Coding takes ~80%. Meters take ~20%.

how it came to be

The door worked. Starting work was the bottleneck.

Once the local gateway was up, the daily question became: which CLI, which model tier, is Headroom alive, am I about to burn cloud credit by mistake?

grok-tua and tok-tua grew out of caregiver-hours shipping: cloud models helping sketch routing and smoke checks, the local repo holding the real scripts. They were extracted from the monorepo so the launchers could version on their own.

July 2026 — monorepo tooling (v0.2 era). Late July / early August 2026 — public extract. They live in one repo on purpose: each imports a bit of the other.

They are how humans and agents open the ai-gateway door. They are not the door.
